RPS Online 98 — Delete Your Account

In the app (immediate)

Lobby → SETTINGS → DELETE ACCOUNT → CONFIRM DELETE

This permanently deletes the anonymous account this device plays on, along with all its data: tokens, rating, match history, chat messages, and friend and guild membership. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app (by request)

Accounts are anonymous — there is no email or password — so to delete an account without the app, email maoravital752@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my RPS Online 98 account" and include your in-game nickname and, if you have it, your friend code (shown in the game's friends screen). We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Everything: the anonymous account identifier, token balance, rating, match history, chat messages, friends, and guild membership. No data is retained after deletion.
